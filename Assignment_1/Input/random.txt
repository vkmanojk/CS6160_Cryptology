The calm river flowed under a small stone bridge while many people walked along the nearby path. Every child carried a small bag filled with bread, fruit, and water for the long afternoon trip. Birds sang from tall trees as warm sunlight covered the hills and fields. An old farmer smiled kindly while caring for cattle near the barn. After a short rest, the group continued toward a quiet lake where clear water reflected the bright blue sky. Everyone felt happy because the weather remained cool and pleasant throughout the day. Their simple outing created lasting memories and encouraged every person to return again during another holiday with family and friends.
